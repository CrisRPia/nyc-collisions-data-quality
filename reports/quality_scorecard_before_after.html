Interpret the changes carefully. Missing boroughs are derived conditionally on correct GPS. Supplied coordinates, including values outside the NYC domain, are retained. Geographic-domain alerts use the official reference envelope plus an approximate 5 km margin. Usable coordinate coverage is reported separately from coordinate presence.

Potential duplicate events and person/role-total differences remain review items. Accuracy and publication latency are unmeasured. Derived boroughs are excluded from the borough-reference agreement check.

Proposed continuous monitoring

  1. Archive each new snapshot, acquisition time, hashes and reference versions.
  2. Validate the source and rerun assessment, cleanup and scorecard before publishing.
  3. Stop on unreadable inputs or failed provenance checks. Export domain violations as record-level alerts while retaining the records and supplied values.
  4. Track rates by load and month; review alerts weekly and rules monthly with the proposed data steward.

This is a proposal; no scheduled service or organizational roles have been deployed. Thresholds require data-consumer approval.
